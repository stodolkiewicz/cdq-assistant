# CDQ Fraud Guard

## Overview

CDQ Fraud Guard is a service for managing and verifying global payment data. It protects businesses against payment fraud and inaccurate bank account information, helping ensure that transactions are secure and compliant.

Source: https://www.cdq.com/products/cdq-fraud-guard

## The problem it solves

Fraudulent activities such as falsified invoices cause significant financial damage. A critical challenge is identifying bank accounts that do not belong to the declared business partner but to an attacker.

CDQ Fraud Guard addresses this by combining verification and alerting: it uses a shared database of verified bank accounts and known fraud cases contributed by the CDQ Data Sharing community.

## Key features

- **Bank Account Verification** — verifies new bank account information against a shared database of validated bank accounts before the account is used operationally.
- **Trust Score** — assigns a trust score to a bank account based on the number of successful transactions recorded for it. The scoring is customizable to match the organization's risk appetite.
- **Payment Fraud Alerts** — warns community members about potential fraud attacks by maintaining and sharing known fraud cases.
- **Fraud Case Management** — lets users document, manage and look up fraud cases in order to identify critical accounts and prevent fraud.
- **Seamless Integration** — integrates with existing financial systems through an API, so no dedicated user interface is required.

## Benefits

- **Enhanced security** — reduces fraud risk by verifying bank account data against a shared database of validated accounts and known fraud cases.
- **Operational efficiency** — streamlines verification of bank account data and management of fraud cases, saving time and resources.
- **Customizable trust scores** — trust scores can be tuned to the organization's own risk management needs.
- **Community-driven data** — draws on the CDQ Data Sharing community for reliable, up-to-date information on bank accounts and fraud cases.
- **Real-time fraud alerts** — delivers immediate alerts on potential fraud cases, enabling fast response.
